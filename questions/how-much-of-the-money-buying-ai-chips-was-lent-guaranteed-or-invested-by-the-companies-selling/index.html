  1. NOV182026

    Nvidia reports its quarter to October 25, 2026: whether days sales outstanding, receivables over revenue times 91, hold at or above 51.1, the average before July, after 59.6 in the quarter to July 26.

    Part 16: The Seller Holds the Paper Pendingp16-t01Pre-specified

    Question
    In NVIDIA's Form 10-Q for its fiscal quarter ended October 25, 2026, is accounts receivable, net at quarter end, divided by the quarter's revenue and multiplied by 91, at or above 51.1 days?
    Source
    SEC EDGAR, NVIDIA Form 10-Q for the quarter ended October 25, 2026 (balance sheet and statement of income), CIK 0001045810
    Rule
    DeepStack computes days sales outstanding from two figures the 10-Q prints: accounts receivable, net on the condensed consolidated balance sheet at October 25, 2026, and revenue on the condensed consolidated statement of income for the three months then ended, times the 91 days of the quarter, rounded to one decimal. The answer is yes if the figure is 51.1 days or more; no if it is below 51.1. The line is the average of the four quarters before the quarter ended July 26, 2026, computed the same way: 54.1 (July 27, 2025), 53.3 (October 26, 2025), 51.4 (January 25, 2026) and 45.4 (April 26, 2026); the quarter ended July 26, 2026 gave 59.6. The payment terms the filing describes are read alongside and do not decide the test.
    If the source is late, revised or silent
    If the 10-Q is not public on EDGAR by December 15, 2026, the test is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read and what was missing; the next reading is December 31, 2026. If the period the 10-Q covers is not 91 days, the rule uses the number of days its period dates give. Figures are read as first filed; a later amendment does not reopen the test.

    Question
    Does the guarantees note in NVIDIA's Form 10-Q for its fiscal quarter ended October 25, 2026 put the total maximum gross exposure under its guarantees above $108.5 billion?
    Source
    SEC EDGAR, NVIDIA Form 10-Q for the quarter ended October 25, 2026 (notes to the condensed consolidated financial statements, guarantees), CIK 0001045810
    Rule
    Read the total maximum gross exposure the guarantees note gives, including guarantees the note says were signed after the quarter ended, as the table for July 26, 2026 did. The answer is yes if it is above $108.5 billion; no if it is $108.5 billion or below. If the note gives no total, the Result sums the maximum exposures the note lists and says that it summed them. The fair value of the guarantees and the phasing of the SB Energy guarantees are read alongside and do not decide the test.
    If the source is late, revised or silent
    If the 10-Q is not public on EDGAR by December 15, 2026, the test is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read and what was missing; the next reading is December 31, 2026. If the note gives no maximum exposure for a guarantee it describes, the test is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read and what was missing; the next reading is NVIDIA's annual report on Form 10-K for fiscal 2027, by March 31, 2027.
