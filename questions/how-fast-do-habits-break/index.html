    Rule
    Read the release, the slides and the call transcript on Meta's investor-relations site; a stated count of monthly or weekly Muse users, a transaction volume or a fee or subscription revenue figure resolves confirmed, download or install counts alone, or no Muse figure, resolves refuted.
    If the source is late, revised or silent
    If Meta reports after November 30, 2026, or restates the quarter, the test is inconclusive, reviewed by the owner.

    Rule

    If Booking gives no direct-mix figure for the quarter, or reports after November 30, 2026, the test is inconclusive, reviewed by the owner.
