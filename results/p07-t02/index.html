The rule

How DeepStack settles a question: the specification.

Question
Did Japan's holdings of U.S. Treasury securities at the end of August 2026, in the Treasury International Capital table of major foreign holders published in mid-October, read higher than at the end of July 2026?
Source
U.S. Treasury, TIC Table 5: Major Foreign Holders of Treasury Securities (monthly text table)
Rule
Compare Japan's August 2026 line with its July 2026 line in the same table. The answer is yes if the August figure is higher; no if it is equal or lower.
If the source is late, revised or silent
A table published after November 30, 2026, or a July figure revised in the same table so that the comparison changes sign, is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read and what was missing.

Approved by Marco Cauduro on October 12, 2026.
