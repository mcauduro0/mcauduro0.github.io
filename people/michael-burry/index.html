Person · Hedge-fund investor

Michael Burry

Michael Burry is the most persistent skeptic in The Buildout. He sees circular financing creating backlogs that are real in paperwork and weak in economics, names the off-balance-sheet structures and non-cancellable contracts “the perimeter of debt,” reads the Nebius rental spread as a market pricing fast decay, counts $2.7 trillion of commitments across five hyperscalers, and in Part 10 attacks the depreciation model behind Nvidia’s residual-value slide.
