Torsten Slok

Torsten Slok turns The Buildout’s revenue question into an accounting test: technology’s forecasts roughly double the sector’s operating cash flow to about $2.4 trillion by 2028, while the forecasts of technology’s customers grow far more slowly, so someone has to write the checks. Hyperscaler credit, in his reading, rests on cash from operations tripling or better by 2030, and total factor productivity has not picked up since the spending cycle began.
