Michael Howell

Michael Howell reads the rate story more kindly than most voices in The Buildout. In Part 07 he estimates nominal output expanding at roughly 7% to 8%, measures the supply-linked term premium at minus 27 basis points and turned buyer when nominal yields rose above 5% and real yields neared 3%. In Part 10 he says he underestimated AI spending’s lift to earnings, calls the entry point the best in years and says past cycles began to buckle near 5.5%.
