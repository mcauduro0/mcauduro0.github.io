Anthropic

Anthropic appears in The Buildout as the fast laboratory: 142% quarterly growth to $11.6 billion, a signed $1.25 billion-a-month compute contract with SpaceX through May 2029, a combined $105 billion run rate with OpenAI in July, and a listing that investors expected at $2 trillion or more. The open questions are concentration (two customers supplied almost 25% of 2025 revenue) and whether audited accounts show durable margins.
