Meta

Meta is the hyperscaler The Buildout follows from both sides of the ledger: charged for financing in Part 04 (earnings down 13%, the stock 22% below its high), building a $27 billion Louisiana campus through an off-balance-sheet vehicle it owns 20% of in Part 08, and rewarded with an 18% gain in Part 09 after releasing Muse, an assistant designed to run errands. Its September-quarter report is a dated test in Parts 09 and 10.
