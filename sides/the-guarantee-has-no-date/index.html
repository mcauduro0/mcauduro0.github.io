    Source

  2. Michael BurrySep 19, 2026

    A purpose-built data center has no deep resale or sublease market, unlike an office, so capacity nobody needs gets written down, as Meta's 2022 charges on offices and data centers showed.

    Source

  3. Scott GallowayJul 27, 2026

    Investors are paying too little attention to the obligations off the balance sheet that sit under Big Tech's AI infrastructure spending.

The case against this side, chosen by hand

Haim ZaltzmanSep 29, 2026

Guarantees and credit support are probably what it takes to clear deals of this size at this speed, as wrappers and insurance do for car and aircraft finance.

This side reads the guarantee as debt kept off the books. Zaltzman reads the same tool as the price of financing a deal this large at all.

The case against this side, chosen by hand

Harley BassmanSep 17, 2026

The hyperscalers, not the labs, do the borrowing, so a lab's failure would be an equity problem rather than a bond problem; the hyperscalers have the cash to pay their debt.

This side follows the loss to the weakest tenant. Bassman answers that the borrowers are the hyperscalers, whose cash keeps a lab's failure out of the bond market.

Source

RVG
Residual value guarantee, a promise to pay the shortfall if a leased building is worth less than an agreed amount when the lease ends.
VIE
Variable interest entity, a vehicle a company may control without owning a majority, which decides whether it must consolidate it.
NAIC
National Association of Insurance Commissioners, the body of state insurance regulators that reviews the ratings insurers rely on.
