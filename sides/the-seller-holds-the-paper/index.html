ARR
Annualized recurring revenue: a month's or a quarter's revenue scaled to a year, not audited revenue.
TPU
Tensor Processing Unit, the AI accelerator Google designs with Broadcom.
neocloud
A cloud company that rents out AI computing, such as CoreWeave or Nebius, as distinct from the large hyperscalers.
