THE CALL (DeepStack's house view, in words)
Stance: Sell habit, buy the rails
Line: Agents will drain weak consumer habits first. Back the rails (trust, records, distribution, compute), not the forgotten subscription.
What would change the Call:
- Meta reports agent downloads without retained users, transaction volume or fees.
- The first tax season with agents shows households still paying Intuit for certainty.
The Call expects no particular answer.
