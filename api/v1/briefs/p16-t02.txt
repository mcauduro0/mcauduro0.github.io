THE QUESTION
Does the guarantees note in NVIDIA's Form 10-Q for its fiscal quarter ended October 25, 2026 put the total maximum gross exposure under its guarantees above $108.5 billion?

HOW IT IS SETTLED
Rule: Read the total maximum gross exposure the guarantees note gives, including guarantees the note says were signed after the quarter ended, as the table for July 26, 2026 did. The answer is yes if it is above $108.5 billion; no if it is $108.5 billion or below. If the note gives no total, the Result sums the maximum exposures the note lists and says that it summed them. The fair value of the guarantees and the phasing of the SB Energy guarantees are read alongside and do not decide the test.
Source: SEC EDGAR, NVIDIA Form 10-Q for the quarter ended October 25, 2026 (notes to the condensed consolidated financial statements, guarantees), CIK 0001045810 (https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001045810&type=10-Q)
If the reading is unclear: If the 10-Q is not public on EDGAR by December 15, 2026, the test is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read and what was missing; the next reading is December 31, 2026. If the note gives no maximum exposure for a guarantee it describes, the test is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read and what was missing; the next reading is NVIDIA's annual report on Form 10-K for fiscal 2027, by March 31, 2027.
