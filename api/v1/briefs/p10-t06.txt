THE QUESTION
Does the C Fund of the Thrift Savings Plan, the federal employees' S&P 500 index fund, close above $126.1817, its highest share price through October 12, 2026 (set on October 6, 2026), on any business day from October 13, 2026 through July 12, 2027?

HOW IT IS SETTLED
Rule: Read the C Fund's daily share prices from October 13, 2026 through July 12, 2027 in the plan's share price history. The answer is yes if any of them is above $126.1817, the highest close through October 12, 2026 (October 6; the plan posted no price on October 12, a federal holiday); no if every one is at or below it. The fund holds the stocks of the S&P 500 and reinvests their dividends, so its share price is the record the test reads.
Source: Thrift Savings Plan, share price history of the C Fund (daily closing share prices, tsp.gov) (https://www.tsp.gov/share-price-history/)
If the reading is unclear: If the plan has not posted the prices through July 12, 2027 by July 19, 2027, the test is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read, what was missing and the day of the next reading. A price the plan corrects is read as corrected.
Test date: 2027-07-12 (By July 12, 2027); the test closes at 00:00 UTC that day

THE PAGE: Part 10, "The Bubble Is in the Denominator"
