THE QUESTION
Did the combined depreciation and amortization of Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta and Amazon for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 grow faster from the year-earlier quarter than their combined operating income?

HOW IT IS SETTLED
Rule: Sum depreciation and amortization, and sum operating income, for the four companies for the September quarter of 2026 and of 2025, from each release or 10-Q. The answer is yes if the year-over-year growth rate of depreciation is above the rate of operating income; no if it is equal or below. A change to server useful lives disclosed in the same filings is DeepStack's reading into the Result, not the answer.
Source: SEC EDGAR, the four companies' earnings releases (Form 8-K) and quarterly reports (Form 10-Q) (https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/)
If the reading is unclear: If any of the four has not reported by November 7, 2026, or restates the year-earlier quarter, the test is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read and what was missing.
Test date: 2026-10-28 (Late October); the test closes at 00:00 UTC that day

THE PAGE: Part 10, "The Bubble Is in the Denominator"
The S&P 500 looks cheaper because profits surged. But those profits depend on AI spending, chip values and debt that may be inflating the denominator while raising the discount rate.
What the page says this test would show: Microsoft and Alphabet report October 28, after Meta on October 21, and Amazon follows on October 29; depreciation, server lives and 29.5% growth will test the earnings denominator.

THE CALL (DeepStack's house view, in words)
