THE QUESTION
In NVIDIA's Form 10-Q for its fiscal quarter ended October 25, 2026, is accounts receivable, net at quarter end, divided by the quarter's revenue and multiplied by 91, at or above 51.1 days?

HOW IT IS SETTLED
Rule: DeepStack computes days sales outstanding from two figures the 10-Q prints: accounts receivable, net on the condensed consolidated balance sheet at October 25, 2026, and revenue on the condensed consolidated statement of income for the three months then ended, times the 91 days of the quarter, rounded to one decimal. The answer is yes if the figure is 51.1 days or more; no if it is below 51.1. The line is the average of the four quarters before the quarter ended July 26, 2026, computed the same way: 54.1 (July 27, 2025), 53.3 (October 26, 2025), 51.4 (January 25, 2026) and 45.4 (April 26, 2026); the quarter ended July 26, 2026 gave 59.6. The payment terms the filing describes are read alongside and do not decide the test.
Source: SEC EDGAR, NVIDIA Form 10-Q for the quarter ended October 25, 2026 (balance sheet and statement of income), CIK 0001045810 (https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001045810&type=10-Q)
If the reading is unclear: If the 10-Q is not public on EDGAR by December 15, 2026, the test is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read and what was missing; the next reading is December 31, 2026. If the period the 10-Q covers is not 91 days, the rule uses the number of days its period dates give. Figures are read as first filed; a later amendment does not reopen the test.
Test date: 2026-11-18 (November 18); the test closes at 00:00 UTC that day

Ciena carries a $10 billion order book, Microsoft's far backlog grew three times as fast as its near one, and Nvidia now waits a year to be paid. The orders will ship. DeepStack expects the profit to arrive later, and the third-quarter reports to show the gap.

THE CALL (DeepStack's house view, in words)
Stance: Revenue yes, margin not yet
Line: The orders will ship. The profit comes later, and a margin that lags the ramp will be reported as a demand miss.
What would change the Call:
- Microsoft's within-a-year backlog grows as fast as the beyond-a-year book on October 28.
- Nvidia's days sales outstanding give back the 14 days Eisman counted, or its terms for investment-grade customers return to 90 days.
- Perma-Pipe converts $57 million or more at a gross margin above 30% while Lumentum and Coherent report their ramps at target margin.
The Call expects the answer yes.
