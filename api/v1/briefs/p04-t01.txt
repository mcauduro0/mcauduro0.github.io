THE QUESTION
Did Alphabet shares close higher on the first full trading day after Alphabet disclosed a 2027 capital-spending budget above its 2026 budget than at the close before the disclosure?

HOW IT IS SETTLED
Rule: Read the 2027 capital-expenditure figure or range Alphabet gives in the release or on the call, and the Nasdaq close of GOOGL on the first full trading day after it against the close before. The answer is yes if the 2027 budget is above 2026's and the close is higher; no if the close is lower or unchanged.
Source: Alphabet investor relations, quarterly earnings release and call (https://abc.xyz/investor/)
If the reading is unclear: If Alphabet gives no 2027 figure by February 15, 2027, or guides 2027 at or below 2026, the test is inconclusive, and the Result says what was read and what was missing.
Test date: 2027-01-01 (2027 budget); the test closes at 00:00 UTC that day

THE CALL (DeepStack's house view, in words)

What would change the Call:
- Alphabet’s larger 2027 budget lifts the stock instead of sinking it.
- Operating cash flow catches capex growth and the labs fund themselves on durable, non-circular revenue.
