How to read these charts

  • Reported figures come from the stories and carry the source and date shown under each chart.
  • Implied values are hatched and labeled. They are derived from a reported growth rate, never guessed.
  • Calculators and scenarios are DeepStack arithmetic on reported figures. They show sensitivities, not forecasts.
  • Live charts carry a Live tag and the time of the last update. They are refreshed from U.S. Treasury; BLS; Federal Reserve H.15, Federal Reserve Board, BLS, BEA, DOL, Census Bureau, University of Michigan, OMB, S&P Dow Jones Indices, Cboe, EIA and IMF via FRED; Trading Economics for household inflation expectations, European gas, European power, uranium, lithium and forecast paths each time the site is rebuilt, not tick by tick.
  • Not advice. The Data Desk is analysis and opinion, not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
