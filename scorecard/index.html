Brier score: not shown until ten results carry a probability recorded before the date (0 so far). From the tenth result whose specification carried a probability before the date, the Scorecard prints the Brier score: the mean of the squared gap between the probability the Call gave the yes answer and what happened, where 0 is perfect and 0.25 is a coin flip; a public consensus is printed beside it where one exists.
