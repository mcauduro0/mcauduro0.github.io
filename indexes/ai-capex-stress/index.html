An index is the weighted mean of the Call’s numbers that stand on the day, each read on the side the index measures (a test read on its no counts 100 minus the number); a number stands only when it was recorded before its test’s date, and a test leaves on its date or the day it resolved. With fewer than two standing numbers the index prints no value. Analysis and opinion, not investment advice.
